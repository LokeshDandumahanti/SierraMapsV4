Chapter 1 : The pain and problem

A few months ago, We were working on an e-commerce website that needed basic location services — geocoding, routes for delivery, nearby places. Just the essentials.

So we integrated Google Maps. Everything worked great... until we checked the billing dashboard.

  • 📈 ₹1.85 Lakh for 1 million requests/month
  • 💳 Credit card required to even begin
  • ⛔️ Free tier? Only 1% of what I actually needed
----------------------------------------------------
Chapter 2 : The Alternatives

Then we tried Mapbox. Slightly cheaper — but complex rate limiting, hidden quota resets, and expensive usage beyond a tiny free tier.

Why is it that simple APIs like converting addresses or fetching directions come with enterprise-grade pricing?

Core Features of SierraMaps

  • 🌍 Geocoding: Convert any address to precise coordinates
  • 📍 Reverse Geocoding: Get readable addresses from latitude & longitude
  • 🛣️ Routing & Directions: Accurate routes with support for multiple waypoints
  • 🏙️ Places API: Search and explore points of interest (POIs), landmarks
  • 🏔️ Elevation API: Retrieve elevation data for any geographic point
  • 📊 Spatial Data API: Access place metadata, categories, and attributes
  • 🗺️ Tile API Server: Build your own maps using internal tile rendering (no external dependencies)
  • 🕒 TimeZone API: Get time zone and local time from any location
